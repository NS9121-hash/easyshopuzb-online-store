    ↓ [Send image + price]
    ↓

    ↓ [Download from Telegram, upload to Supabase]
    ↓

    ↓ [Real-time update via Realtime]
    ↓

    ↓

    ↓ [Fill order form]
    ↓
Order API
    ↓ [Save to database]
    ↓ [Send notification]
    ↓
Order Bot (You see order)
